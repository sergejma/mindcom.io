How it works

  1. 1

    Install Dials on your iPhone and your Apple Watch.

  2. 2

    Sign in to WHOOP once on your iPhone.

  3. 3

    Add the complications to your face. The app shows you where each one goes.

Your watch then loads new numbers by itself, about every 30 minutes.

Your data

Your numbers go from WHOOP straight to your Apple Watch. No server of ours stores them. Only the sign-in passes through a small server that keeps nothing.

  • No account.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking.

Dials only reads, nothing changes in your WHOOP account. Disconnect anytime in Settings, and Dials deletes everything on your iPhone and watch. Privacy policy

Questions

Do I need a WHOOP membership?

Yes. Dials reads your data from WHOOP. Without a membership and a WHOOP wearable there is nothing to show.

Which Apple Watch do I need?

Any Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later. Modular Ultra and Wayfinder are on Apple Watch Ultra. Modular Duo, Infograph and the Smart Stack work on other models too.

How fresh are the numbers?

Dials shows what WHOOP already has. Your watch loads new numbers about every 30 minutes; watchOS decides the exact moment. Recovery and Sleep appear once WHOOP has scored your night. If something looks old, open the WHOOP app on your iPhone so your WHOOP can sync, then open Dials on your watch.

What does it cost?

Nothing. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription.

Is Dials made by WHOOP?

No. Dials is an independent app by Sergej Markwart. It uses the WHOOP API and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WHOOP.
