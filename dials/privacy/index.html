Privacy

Dials · Last updated: October 31, 2026

Dials shows your WHOOP data on your Apple Watch.

Your data

Your data goes directly from WHOOP to your devices.

Sign-in

The WHOOP sign-in runs through a small server. It only adds the app’s WHOOP key to the sign-in request and stores nothing: no tokens, no health data, no logs with personal data.

On your devices

The app keeps only the latest values, no history. iOS and watchOS store them encrypted. They are deleted when you disconnect or delete the app.

No tracking

No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no sharing with third parties.

WHOOP

WHOOP’s own privacy policy applies to your WHOOP account. Dials is an independent app and is not affiliated with WHOOP. Dials uses the WHOOP API.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other servers.
